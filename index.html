About Dola Paribahan

Pioneering seamless connectivity across southern Bangladesh

25+ Years of Dedicated Service

Established in 2001, Dola Paribahan has remained a trusted inter-district bus operator connecting Dhaka directly with Gopalganj, Pirojpur, Bagerhat, and Khulna.

With the opening of the Padma Bridge, Dola Paribahan revolutionized southern travel time, offering modern Isuzu Non-AC and Deluxe AC coaches equipped with plush reclining seating, GPS tracking, and experienced highway drivers.
